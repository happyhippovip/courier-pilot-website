AI work stalls. A provider limit hits mid-task. A laptop sleeps. A process dies overnight. The context, the checkpoint, the half-finished run — gone, and the retry starts blind.

Courier Symphony treats that as the normal case, not the exception: durable checkpoints, capability-aware routing, and recovery that resumes instead of repeating.

What it does

One layer that keeps long-running AI work durable, bounded, and recoverable.

Durable continuity and checkpointing
Work survives session ends, restarts, and host failures. Recovery resumes from the last checkpoint.
Capability-aware routing
Each unit of work goes to an execution environment that can actually do it — by provider, machine, and tool.
Host-aware bounded capacity
Concurrency follows what the machine can bear. Small hosts stay responsive; scale comes from more hosts, not more load.
Safe parallelism
One writer per mutable scope. Parallel work that cannot collide, instead of hope and retries.
Recovery, not repetition
After provider limits or host and process failures, work continues — never a blind duplicate execution of the same effect.
Human gates
Spending, credentials, and sensitive actions always stop at a person. Unknown authorization fails closed.

How it works

  1. 1

    Checkpoint

    Progress, decisions, and durable state are recorded as work runs — including across machines.

  2. 2

    Route

    Each next step is assigned to a capable, available environment. Nothing runs where it cannot succeed.

  3. 3

    Recover

    When a provider, machine, or process fails, the work resumes from its checkpoint on a healthy path.

Safety by construction

  • No autonomous spending. Ever.
  • No credentials or secrets leave their vault.
  • No duplicate side effects from retries.
  • Every sensitive action waits for a human decision.
