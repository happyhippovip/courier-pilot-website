Why Courier Exists

Modern AI models write remarkable code, but unattended execution breaks down in practice:

The Fragility of Sessions

When a laptop sleeps, an API connection drops, or a terminal closes, hours of agent trajectory are lost. Courier Symphony records every transition in an append-only ledger so execution resumes without re-running finished work.

The Danger of Guesswork

When an agent encounters an unknown status, standard systems blindly retry or guess. Courier Symphony enforces fail-closed execution: if evidence is incomplete or ambiguous, the process halts safely and logs the discrepancy.

Sovereign Cost & Safety Control

No agent is ever permitted to authorize spending, invoke external credit cards, or expose credentials autonomously. Human approval gates are strictly enforced at runtime boundaries.

Technical Lane Architecture

Our engineering roadmap progresses across structured architectural lanes:

    Lane 1 — Golden Baselines & Regressions
    Strict regression ratchets, zero new test exclusions, and end-to-end failover tests.
    Lane 2 — Execution Ledger & Work Queue
    Append-only JSONL ledgers, time-bounded leases, receipt read models, and consistency audit engines.
    Lane 3 — Host Admission & Process Governance
    Hardware capacity checks, single-heavy-job host locks, Win32 Job Objects, and POSIX process session isolation.
    Lane 4 — Reality Analysis & Code Scans
    Repo Reality Check engine, AST validation, and tamper-evident audit reporting.
    Lane 5 — Coordination Hub & Read-Only MCP
    Task coordination protocols, multi-agent missions, and secure read-only MCP endpoints.
